## About the company — short
Floating City Limited is a UK-based maritime innovation and sustainable urban-development company exploring the next generation of floating cities, ocean-based tourism, Blue Economy infrastructure and sustainable maritime communities. Its proposed projects bring together marine engineering, hospitality, residential and commercial development, smart technologies, cultural heritage and international collaboration.

## About the company — long
Floating City Limited envisions a future in which living, working, travelling, investing and retiring are no longer confined to conventional land-based destinations. Through a portfolio of complementary maritime concepts, the company aims to develop ocean-going urban communities, supporting maritime infrastructure and new opportunities for global tourism, business, research and sustainable development. Its work spans City At Sea™, an ambitious large-scale mobile floating-city vision; Muziris City At Sea, an India-focused floating smart-city concept inspired by Kerala’s maritime history; a proposed Muziris Port development in the Kochi region; and a smaller World Voyage concept exploring life and hospitality aboard a converted cruise vessel. Each initiative remains subject to feasibility, financing, engineering, regulatory review and development decisions.

## Vision
To help shape a future in which ocean-based infrastructure connects people, businesses and cultures through sustainable living, mobility and maritime innovation.

## Mission
To advance floating urban concepts through responsible marine engineering, smart infrastructure, international partnerships and commercially viable development models.

## Values
Safety-first engineering; environmental responsibility; transparency; innovation; collaboration; accessibility; cultural exchange; long-term resilience.

## Design direction
Premium, cinematic, architectural, restrained, future-facing and trustworthy. Logo-inspired palette: deep navy `#071B30`, ocean blue `#123C59`, cyan `#70CADB`, sea mist `#DCEFF2`, pearl white `#F7F9FA`, optional muted champagne `#C2A36B`. # 07. MUZIRIS CITY AT SEA PROJECT PAGE

**URL:** `/projects/muziris-city-at-sea`

**SEO title:** Muziris City At Sea | Proposed Floating Smart City

**Meta description:** Discover the India-focused Muziris City At Sea concept, its preliminary 2035 engineering vision, smart infrastructure, sustainability strategy and development framework.

## Hero
**Eyebrow:** INDIA'S MARITIME HERITAGE. A FUTURE-FACING VISION.

**Headline:** Where Maritime History Inspires Tomorrow.

**Copy:** Inspired by the historic Muziris trade gateway, Muziris City At Sea proposes an ocean-going smart community integrating residences, commerce, hospitality, technology and maritime innovation.

## Identity
Muziris City At Sea is the **floating vessel/community concept**, not the separate coastal Muziris Port development. Its relationship to the wider City At Sea™ / Freedomship design family must be clarified by the client before definitive engineering claims are made.

## Updated Technical Specifications — 2035 Vision
**Source:** Muziris City At Sea project report, Chapter 8. **All values conceptual.**

- Overall length: **1,399.4 m**.
- Length at waterline: **1,308.6 m**.
- Beam: **230 m**.
- Overall width: **242.9 m**.
- Overall height including radar mast: **190.9 m**.
- Height to flight deck: **110.6 m**.
- Design draft: approximately **14–15 m**, subject to final design.
- Proposed speed: **25 knots**.
- Target design life: **100 years** (design objective, not a warranty).
- Classification society and flag: **to be determined**.

## Integrated Masterplan
The concept includes residential accommodation, hotels, serviced apartments, retail, offices, convention and exhibition facilities, medical and wellness services, education and research, cultural venues, recreation, marina facilities and marine support systems.

## Engineering & Smart Infrastructure
Proposed studies and systems include hull optimisation, hydrostatics, hydrodynamics, stability and seakeeping; structural analysis and fatigue assessment; propulsion and power distribution; HVAC, water treatment, fire protection, communications, navigation and cybersecurity; digital twins, IoT monitoring and predictive maintenance.

## Environmental Strategy
The programme considers energy efficiency, renewable integration where feasible, responsible water treatment and recycling, waste minimisation, lifecycle optimisation and marine ecosystem protection. Environmental outcomes require detailed assessment and verification.

## Construction Philosophy
The report explores modular fabrication across multiple facilities, including hull blocks, accommodation, commercial, hospital, machinery, utilities and airport-deck modules, followed by systems integration and testing.

## Development Pathway
Concept definition → feasibility and environmental assessment → preliminary engineering → detailed engineering and approvals → construction planning → fabrication/integration → harbour and sea trials → certification → operations if approved.

## Investment Framework
The detailed project report outlines a **preliminary US$25 billion** development framework including a proposed **US$8 billion mega shipyard**. Figures are planning estimates and not proof of funding or a final investment decision.

## Indicative Construction Duration
The report presents a **5–7-year programme** for engineering, shipyard preparation, fabrication, integration and trials, conditional on financing, approvals and technical feasibility. It also discusses longer-range milestones; no completion date is guaranteed.
