Muziris – City At Sea is presented as an attempt to move beyond the conventional cruise-ship model.

A conventional cruise ship primarily provides:

- transportation;
- tourism;
- temporary accommodation;
- hospitality;
- entertainment.

The Muziris concept instead combines elements of:

- a large ocean-going vessel;
- a residential community;
- a smart city;

- a commercial district;
- a healthcare ecosystem;
- an education ecosystem;
- an entertainment destination;
- an aviation and marine mobility hub;
- a business and innovation centre;
- a sustainability platform;
- an international travelling community.

The underlying idea is that a resident should be able to remain within one urban ecosystem while the ecosystem itself moves internationally.
