# 2. Core Vision

The project is presented as a **large-scale floating smart city** rather than simply a cruise ship or floating hotel.

Its proposed purpose is to create an environment where people can:

**Live → Work → Travel → Retire → Study → Shop → Receive Healthcare → Entertain → Conduct Business**

while the overall structure continuously travels around the world.

The three major identity pillars presented in the project material are:

### Continuous Global Voyage

The city is intended to operate as a continuously travelling maritime community rather than remaining permanently anchored in one location.

### 100,000+ Safety Capacity

The overall project is designed around a very large population of residents, visitors and crew.

### Green & Sustainable by Design

The concept incorporates renewable energy, water recycling, smart infrastructure and other technologies intended to reduce environmental impact.

# 4. What Exactly Is Muziris – City At Sea?

The concept is essentially a **floating urban ecosystem**.

Instead of creating separate buildings on land, the project proposes putting many functions of a conventional city into one enormous maritime structure.

Conceptually:

```text
                    MUZIRIS
```
